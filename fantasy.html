Fantasy Anime

Fantasy anime transports viewers into imaginative worlds filled with magic, mythical creatures, powerful kingdoms, and unforgettable adventures. These stories follow determined heroes, unlikely friendships, and epic journeys that test courage and identity—perfect for anyone who wants to escape into something bigger than real life.

Black Clover Action, Fantasy Crunchyroll
Hulu 		Asta is born without magic in a world where magic defines status and power. Determined to become the Wizard King, he trains relentlessly alongside his gifted rival Yuno. After joining the Magic Knights, Asta proves that grit can compete with destiny as he faces powerful enemies, rival kingdoms, and dark forces threatening the Clover Kingdom.

Main characters: Asta, Yuno, Noelle Silva

If you like: Harry Potter or The Witcher, you’ll love this underdog magic adventure.
Ascendance of a Bookworm Fantasy, Slice of Life Crunchyroll A book-loving college student is reborn in a medieval fantasy world where books are rare and only the wealthy can access them. Now living as Myne, she is physically weak but mentally determined. Using modern knowledge, she tries to create books from scratch while learning about magic, social class, and survival in a world that doesn’t make curiosity easy.

Main characters: Myne, Lutz, Ferdinand

If you like: Bridgerton or Little Women, you’ll enjoy this cozy, smart fantasy world.
Noragami Action, Fantasy Crunchyroll Yato is a broke minor god who dreams of becoming famous. After an accident connects high schooler Hiyori to the spirit world, she gets pulled into Yato’s chaotic life. With his weapon partner Yukine, Yato takes odd jobs for five yen while battling spirits, rival gods, and the consequences of his hidden past.

Main characters: Yato, Hiyori Iki, Yukine

If you like: Supernatural or Lucifer, you’ll love this fast-paced urban fantasy.
Delicious in Dungeon Fantasy, Comedy Netflix After a dungeon raid goes wrong, Laios and his party have no time and no money to spare. To rescue their friend, they push deeper into the dungeon—surviving by cooking and eating the monsters they defeat. With Senshi leading the “monster cuisine,” the show mixes classic fantasy adventure, comedy, and surprisingly thoughtful world-building.

Main characters: Laios, Marcille, Senshi

If you like: Dungeons & Dragons or The Great British Bake Off, you’ll enjoy this quirky adventure.
